# Tenancy Agreement — Standard Terms

This is a plain-language summary of the standard tenancy agreement (ভাড়ার চুক্তি) that every tenant signs with the owner, Abdur Rahman. The signed paper agreement is the final word if anything here differs.

## Rent and service charge

The monthly rent and the service charge are written in your lease. The service charge covers the caretaker, night guard, cleaner, garbage collection, lift and generator maintenance, common-area electricity and the Titas gas bill. Rent and service charge are paid together as one amount each month.

## Rent due date and how to pay (ভাড়া পরিশোধ)

Rent must be paid by the 7th of each month for that month. You can pay by bKash or Nagad to the owner's merchant number, by bank transfer, or in cash to the owner or caretaker against a signed receipt. Always keep the bKash/Nagad transaction ID (TrxID); the payment is recorded in BariBhara against your flat and month.

## Late fee (দেরিতে ভাড়া)

If the rent is not paid by the 10th of the month, a late fee of ৳500 is added for that month. If rent for two months in a row remains unpaid, the owner may give written notice to end the tenancy. If you know you will be late, talk to the owner before the 7th; a short extension is usually possible once or twice a year.

## Advance / security deposit (অগ্রিম)

The advance is equal to two months of basic rent (without service charge), paid when the lease is signed. The advance does not earn interest and cannot be used as the last months' rent unless the owner agrees in writing.

## Notice period (নোটিশ)

To leave the flat, the tenant must give two months' written notice, normally by the 1st of the month. For example, notice given on 1 March means the flat is handed over by 30 April. The owner must also give two months' written notice to a tenant who has followed the agreement, except for serious breaches such as unpaid rent or illegal activity.

## Advance refund and deductions

The advance is refunded within 7 days of handing over the keys. The owner may deduct: unpaid rent, late fees or utility bills; the cost of repairing damage beyond normal wear and tear (broken fittings, holes in walls, stained floors); and the cost of repainting if walls were painted a different colour without permission. Normal wear and tear, such as faded paint or small nail holes, is not deducted. A written list of any deductions is given with the refund.

## Rent increase

Rent may be increased only at lease renewal and not more than once every two years, unless both sides agree otherwise. The owner gives at least three months' written notice of any increase. Service charge may be adjusted once a year if utility or salary costs change, with one month's notice.

## Repairs and alterations

The owner pays for major repairs: plumbing lines, electrical wiring, the lift, the water pump, roof leaks and structural problems. The tenant pays for damage they cause and for small consumables such as light bulbs. Do not make holes for an AC, change locks or do any construction without written permission. Report repairs through BariBhara or to the caretaker.

## Subletting

Subletting the flat or a room, or using the flat as an office or mess, is not allowed without the owner's written permission.
