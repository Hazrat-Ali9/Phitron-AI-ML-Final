# Emergency Contacts

Who to call in an emergency at BariBhara buildings. Save these numbers in your phone. জরুরি নম্বর।

## National emergency numbers (Bangladesh)

- **999**: National Emergency Service. Free from any phone, 24 hours, for police, fire service and ambulance.
- **16163**: Fire Service and Civil Defence hotline.
- **109**: Helpline for violence against women and children.
- **333**: Government information and services helpline.

If there is a fire, gas leak with a strong smell, serious injury or a crime in progress, call 999 first, then the caretaker.

## Gas leak or fire

For a gas smell: turn off all burners and the main gas valve, open doors and windows, do not use any switch, lighter or mobile phone inside the flat, leave the flat and call the caretaker and the Titas Gas emergency line. For a fire: leave by the stairs, never the lift; close doors behind you; call 999 or 16163. Every floor has a fire extinguisher next to the lift.

## Lift stuck

Stay calm and press the alarm button inside the lift. Call the caretaker; the lift company is called by the caretaker. Do not try to force the doors open.
