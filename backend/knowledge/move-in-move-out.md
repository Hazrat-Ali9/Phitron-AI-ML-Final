# Move-In and Move-Out Guide

Checklists for starting and ending a tenancy in a BariBhara building. বাসায় ওঠা ও বাসা ছাড়া।

## Before moving in (বাসায় ওঠার আগে)

1. Sign the tenancy agreement and pay the advance (two months' basic rent) and the first month's rent plus service charge.
2. Give the caretaker a copy of the NID of every adult in the family and a passport-size photo. The owner submits the tenant information form to the local police station, as required in Dhaka.
3. Agree the move-in date and time with the caretaker. Moving furniture is allowed from 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM; the lift can be booked for moving, and you must protect the lift walls.

## Move-in inspection and meter readings

On the first day, walk through the flat with the caretaker and note anything already damaged: cracked tiles, broken switches, stains, leaking taps, missing keys. Take dated photos. Write down the prepaid electricity meter balance; the balance left by the previous tenant belongs to the owner and is settled separately. Check that the gas burners, geyser, taps, flush and all lights work. Both sides sign the inspection list; it is used again at move-out.

## Keys (চাবি)

You receive two sets of flat keys, one main-gate key and, if allotted, a parking card. Changing the flat lock needs the owner's permission, and a copy of the new key must be given to the owner in a sealed envelope for emergencies. Lost gate keys cost ৳300 to replace.

## Giving notice to leave

Give two months' written notice to the owner, normally by the 1st of the month (see the Tenancy Agreement — Standard Terms). Pay rent for the notice period as usual. Tell the caretaker your moving date so the lift can be booked.

## Before moving out (বাসা ছাড়ার আগে)

1. Pay all rent, service charge and any late fees up to the last day.
2. Recharge or settle the prepaid electricity meter so the balance is not negative, and pay the internet provider and cut the connection.
3. Remove all your belongings, clean the flat, the kitchen and the bathrooms, and remove nails, hooks and wall stickers you added.
4. Do the move-out inspection with the caretaker using the move-in list and photos.

## Painting expectations (রং)

Normal wear, such as slightly faded paint and small nail holes, is expected and is not charged. If you stayed more than two years, the owner repaints at their own cost. If you stayed less than two years and the walls are heavily marked, drawn on, or were painted another colour, the cost of repainting those walls is deducted from the advance.

## Key handover and advance refund

Return all keys, the gate key and the parking card to the owner or caretaker and sign the handover form. The advance is refunded within 7 days of the handover, minus any deductions listed in writing (unpaid dues and damage beyond normal wear and tear).
