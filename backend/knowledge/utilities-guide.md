# Utilities Guide — Electricity, Gas, Water and Internet

How the utilities work in BariBhara buildings and who pays for what. বিদ্যুৎ, গ্যাস, পানি ও ইন্টারনেট।

## Electricity: prepaid meter (প্রিপেইড মিটার)

Each flat has its own prepaid electricity meter. Rahman Villa (Mirpur) and Shanti Neer (Uttara) are on DESCO; Nur Manzil (Dhanmondi) is on DPDC. The tenant pays for their own electricity by recharging the meter; it is not part of the rent or service charge.

To recharge with bKash: open the bKash app, choose Pay Bill, select DESCO Prepaid (or DPDC Prepaid for Nur Manzil), enter the meter number printed on the meter, the amount and your PIN. Nagad works the same way under Bill Pay. You receive a token by SMS; on most meters the balance updates automatically, otherwise type the 20-digit token into the meter keypad. Keep a few hundred taka of balance, because when the balance reaches zero the power is cut until you recharge. The meter gives a small emergency balance once; it is deducted from your next recharge.

## Gas: Titas line (গ্যাস)

Kitchens have a Titas Gas double-burner line. The monthly Titas gas bill (৳1,080 per double burner) is included in the service charge, so tenants do not pay it separately. Low gas pressure in the morning (7–10 AM) is common in these areas; if the pressure stays very low all day, report it to the caretaker. If you smell gas, turn off the burners and the main gas valve, open the windows, do not switch any light or fan on or off, and call the caretaker or the Titas emergency line.

## Water: WASA (পানি)

Water comes from Dhaka WASA into the underground reservoir and is pumped to the roof tank. The water bill is paid by the owner and covered by the service charge. The pump runs on a schedule (around 6 AM, 12 PM and 6 PM); if your flat has no water outside these times, tell the caretaker. The water tank and reservoir are cleaned every 3 months; you get a notice one day before. Boil or filter water before drinking.

## Generator backup

The building generator starts automatically during a power cut. It powers the lift, the water pump, stair lights and, inside each flat, the lights and fans only. Do not run an AC, geyser, iron, oven or water heater on generator power. Generator diesel and maintenance are covered by the service charge.

## Internet and cable TV

Fibre broadband is available from local ISPs already wired into each building (for example Link3, Carnival and the local area ISP in Mirpur and Uttara). Contact the provider directly; the tenant pays the provider. Ask the caretaker before any new cable is pulled through the walls or windows. Cable/dish TV is also arranged directly with the local operator.

## Utility problems: who to call

For a prepaid meter that will not accept a token, contact DESCO or DPDC customer service. For no gas or low pressure in the whole building, the caretaker contacts Titas. For a broken pump, water leak or generator fault, report it to the caretaker or create a repair request in BariBhara.
