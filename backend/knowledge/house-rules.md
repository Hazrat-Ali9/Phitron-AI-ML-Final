# House Rules — BariBhara Buildings

These rules apply to all three BariBhara buildings: Rahman Villa (Mirpur 10), Nur Manzil (Dhanmondi) and Shanti Neer (Uttara Sector 11). They exist so that every family can live peacefully. বাড়ির নিয়ম সবার জন্য সমান।

## Main gate timings (গেট বন্ধের সময়)

The main gate opens at 6:00 AM and is locked at 11:30 PM every night. The night guard sits at the gate from 10:00 PM to 6:00 AM. If you will come back after 11:30 PM, tell the caretaker before 10:00 PM so the guard can let you in. Do not give the gate key or the gate code to anyone outside your family.

## Guests and overnight stays (অতিথি)

Day visitors are welcome and do not need to be registered. A guest who stays overnight must be registered with the caretaker, Md. Karim Uddin (01819-205577), on the same day. Give the guest's full name, phone number and a photo of their NID or passport. This is a police requirement in Dhaka. A guest may stay up to 7 nights in a row; longer stays need written permission from the owner.

## Noise and quiet hours

Quiet hours are from 10:30 PM to 7:00 AM. During quiet hours do not play loud music or TV, move furniture or do drilling. Construction or repair work inside a flat (drilling, hammering, tiling) is allowed only between 9:00 AM and 6:00 PM, Saturday to Thursday, and never on Friday before 2:00 PM (Jumma prayer time). Parties and family events must end by 10:30 PM; inform the caretaker in advance.

## Garbage collection (ময়লা)

Garbage is collected from every floor once a day at 9:00 AM. Keep your garbage in a tied bag outside your door before 9:00 AM. Do not leave garbage on the stairs, landing or roof, and never throw anything from the balcony. Separate glass and sharp items and tell the cleaner. The monthly garbage collection fee is included in the service charge.

## Pets

Cats and small caged birds are allowed. Dogs need written permission from the owner. Pets must never be left alone in the lift, stairs, garage or other common areas, and owners must clean up after them. A pet that disturbs neighbours repeatedly may have to be removed.

## Rooftop (ছাদ)

The rooftop is open from 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Children under 12 must be with an adult on the roof. Drying clothes on the roof is allowed on the clothes lines only. Do not climb the water tank, and do not hold gatherings or barbecues on the roof without the owner's permission.

## Parking

Car parking is allotted per flat as written in your agreement; use only your own numbered space. Motorbikes and bicycles park on the left side of the ground floor. Do not park in front of the main gate or the generator room. Washing cars inside the garage is allowed only before 8:00 AM using a bucket, not a hose.

## Common areas and safety

Smoking is not allowed in the lift, stairs, lobby or garage. Do not store gas cylinders, kerosene or other flammable things on the stairs or balcony. Keep shoes and bicycles inside your flat, not on the landing. Children must not play with the lift buttons. Report any broken light, leaking pipe or lift problem to the caretaker or through the BariBhara app.
